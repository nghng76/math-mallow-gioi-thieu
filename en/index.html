Why Math Mallow

Three things that make a good lesson

📘

In step with school

Every question is tied to a learning outcome of Vietnam's 2018 national math curriculum. Lessons follow the order of the Kết nối tri thức textbooks, so what kids practise matches what their class is doing.

🧱

Understanding, not memorising

Taught the Singapore way: from real objects, to pictures, and only then to numbers. Number bonds and bar models help kids "see" word problems.

✨

AI grading, kind feedback

Handwritten answers and photos of paper work are graded too. Skill levels come from clear, fixed rules; AI only writes the friendly comments for parents and the praise for kids.

Kid mode

Every lesson is a stage of the adventure

Kids travel along a map where each chapter is a topic from the textbook. Finishing a stage unlocks the next one.

  1. 1
    Look at the lesson pictureSingapore-style illustrations such as number bonds. Short videos hosted by Mallow are being added over time.
  2. 2
    Answer questions at the right levelEach round picks questions for the child: more easy ones while a skill is still shaky, harder ones once it is solid.
  3. 3
    Earn stars and praiseEvery stage ends with a little celebration. No stars yet? Just try again, no telling-off.
  4. 4
    Extra practice or a challengeKids who need more time get extra practice stages; kids who are flying get harder challenge stages.
How kids answer

Many kinds of questions, like real practice

Each type of problem has a fitting way to answer. Most are graded instantly on the device; only handwriting and photos need AI.

✍️HandwritingWrite numbers and column sums right on the screen, like in a notebook.
📷Photo of paper workMulti-step work done on paper, snapped and graded.
🧩Drag and dropFill numbers into number bonds, bar models and number lines.
🔗MatchingMatch sums to answers, shapes to their names.
🔢OrderingPut numbers or objects in order.
🕒Set the clockMove the hands to show the time asked for.
📝Word problemsFill in the sentence, the sum and the answer, or write the whole solution, with a bar model hint.
⚡Mental mathA gentle timer; when time runs out, kids just keep going.
Explore

Math stories and English math words

Between lessons there are fun rest stops, so kids see that math has a history and real people behind it.

  • Picture stories with read-aloud: tales about mathematicians, where numbers came from and Vietnamese folk stories, paged like a real book. A fun question at the end, no score.
  • English math vocabulary: word cards with a picture, pronunciation and an example, then games: bubble pop, memory flip, spelling.
  • Highlighted words inside lessons: tap one to see its meaning and hear it.
  • My word book: reread unlocked stories and replay learned words any time.
Parent mode

Know what your child is good at, and what to practise

More than a score. Parents see their child's level for each learning outcome of the curriculum, grouped by strand.

  • Three clear levels: Not yet · Met · Strong, based on the last 10 attempts for each outcome.
  • Common mistakes: forgetting to carry, reversed digits, wrong sign… so you know where your child gets stuck.
  • Review every question: what your child answered, the right answer, the worked solution, the photo of their work.
💬

A comment after every lesson

A short note as soon as your child finishes: the result, what they just mastered, and anything to keep an eye on.

📅

Weekly, monthly, semester summaries

Compared with the previous period: what improved and what to revisit. In the app, plus email if you turn it on.

🧭

Parents decide on review

When a skill is still weak, the app suggests a personal review stage. It is only added once you agree, or you pick the topics yourself.

📝

Tests like at school

End-of-topic and end-of-semester tests, scored out of 10. Parents can also assign extra tests.

⏱️

Daily time limit

Set minutes per day for each child. The app warns 5 minutes ahead and lets them finish the current stage before locking.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Several kids, one account

Each child gets their own profile and path. Parents choose the grade and the chapter to start from.

Safety & privacy

Designed for children from day one

Your child's data is what parents care about most, so it is what we are most careful with.

🔐 Parent gate

Settings, reports, extra time and leaving kid mode all require the parent PIN, fingerprint or face unlock.

🛡️ Encrypted data

Emails, children's names, PINs and other sensitive details are encrypted before they are stored, so they stay unreadable even if the server is breached.

🙈 AI doesn't know who your child is

For grading, AI only receives the work itself and an internal ID: no name, no face photo, nothing that identifies the child.

🚫 No ads, no random videos

Lesson videos are hosted on our own servers, not YouTube, so there are no ads or suggested videos. Parents can delete the account and all of their child's data at any time.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Are the lessons in English?

The app itself — menus, buttons, parent reports and settings — can be switched to English in Settings. The lessons follow Vietnam's national curriculum, so lesson titles, questions and stories are in Vietnamese for now; kids also learn English math words along the way.

My child's school uses a different textbook. Will it still work?

Yes. Questions are tied to the learning outcomes of the national curriculum, not to one textbook series. Lessons currently follow the Kết nối tri thức order, and parents can choose which chapter their child starts from to match the class.

My child is already in grade 2 or 3. Where do they start?

From grade 2 up, kids take a short starting quiz so the app knows their level. For weak areas the app suggests review stages, and parents decide whether to add them. Grade 1 just has a gentle warm-up that never blocks the way.

What if the AI grades an answer wrongly?

Your child can ask for a regrade. If it is still marked wrong, the question goes to a "needs checking" list where parents see the photo of the work and confirm right or wrong. The parent's decision wins, and stars and skill levels are recalculated.

Can my child learn offline?

Yes. On Wi-Fi the app downloads the next one or two stages in advance. Offline, most questions are still graded on the device; the ones that need AI are graded once the connection is back.

Are there streaks or leaderboards?

No. We chose not to add "learn every day" pressure or comparisons with other kids. Kids earn stars for finishing stages, and have stories and English words to discover — the fun comes from the learning itself.
